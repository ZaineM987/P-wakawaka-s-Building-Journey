Educator Notes: Construction & Pedagogy

This application integrates 50 Core Construction, Architecture, & Trade Concepts based on the standards of the world's leading economies.

Designed for 5-to-6-year-olds, it utilizes Cognitive Load Theory (CLT) by John Sweller to make advanced concepts accessible:

  • Minimizing Extraneous Load: Interfaces are stripped of unnecessary text. The scrolling layout is enforced via Flexbox to ensure native tactile behavior on mobile.
  • Managing Intrinsic Load: Complex ideas (e.g. BIM, Parametric Design, Radiant Heating) are reduced to highly relatable, single-step ECE trivia schemas.
  • Modality Effect: The Text-to-Speech (TTS) feature prevents working memory overload by offloading reading demands to the auditory channel.
